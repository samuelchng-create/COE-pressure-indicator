# October 2026 COE consultation scenario contract

## Official proposal represented

The source is LTA's October 2026 public consultation paper on merging
Categories A and B into one passenger-car COE category and applying an
OMV-percentile fee or rebate to the common COE price.

- **Option A — three bands:** 0th–35th percentile receives a S$15,000 rebate;
  35th–50th receives no adjustment; above the 50th percentile pays a
  S$15,000 surcharge.
- **Option B — five bands:** 0th–10th receives a S$15,000 rebate; 10th–35th
  receives a S$7,500 rebate; 35th–50th receives no adjustment; 50th–75th pays
  a S$7,500 surcharge; above the 75th percentile pays a S$15,000 surcharge.

The percentile shares are registration-weighted as described by LTA. They are
not inferred from the number of models in each example list. Negative values
are rebates and positive values are surcharges.

Source: <https://go.gov.sg/coe-public-consult>

## What existing public data can estimate

For each completed exercise from October 2015 onward, the counterfactual builds
an accounting proxy:

`(Cat A premium × Cat A quota + Cat B premium × Cat B quota) / combined quota`

The next-exercise planning proxy applies the same weights to the existing Cat A
and B structural forecasts, using the announced next-exercise quotas when the
tender identity matches. Both feebate schedules are then applied mechanically
to the selected base proxy.

This permits exact comparison of the published schedules' registration shares,
weighted mean adjustment, mean absolute adjustment, dispersion, largest
adjacent-band step and bottom-to-top effective-cost spread.

## What the projection cannot identify

The public tender table contains final clearing prices, total bids and quotas,
but not individual bid values. It therefore cannot replay a merged auction or
estimate:

- the common clearing price that would have emerged;
- switching between car models or OMV bands;
- bidder price elasticity or dealer absorption of adjustments;
- the effect of redesigning or removing Category E;
- renewal and transition arrangements; or
- realised government receipts.

The quota-weighted series is consequently labelled a proxy throughout. Its
historical volatility is not evidence that an actual merged auction would have
followed the same path. The weighted mean adjustment uses the indicative band
shares and is a distributional calculation, not a revenue forecast.
